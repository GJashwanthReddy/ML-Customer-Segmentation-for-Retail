STRATEGY

Why customer segmentation?

Different customers demonstrate distinct shopping habits. Treating all customers identically leads to inefficient marketing and defection. By grouping shoppers according to actual purchasing behavior, retailers can tailor re-engagement campaigns, maximize customer lifetime value, and protect revenue.

ALGORITHMIC DECISION

K-Means achieves a competitive Silhouette Score while operating in O(N · K · I) time complexity compared to O(N³) for Agglomerative Hierarchical clustering, making it the production model choice for retail customer datasets.
